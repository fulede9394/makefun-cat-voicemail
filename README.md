Entry for the [Funniest Cat Voicemail Greeting](https://make.fun/bounty/u-eager-stork-81-9647-funniest-cat-voicemail-greeting-8eaad443) bounty on make.fun ($2, winner takes all).

Biscuit the cat records his own voicemail greeting: he is asleep on your laptop, and your message will be knocked off later. One line, 112 characters, inside the 1–120 gate.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the greeting to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).

## Entry

> Hi, you've reached Biscuit. I'm asleep on your laptop. Leave a message after the beep — I'll knock it off later.
